## Failure modes

Key symptoms (necessary conditions) are in **bold**; w = weight.

### FM-CAVITATION - Cavitation (HIGH)

- **Occurs in:** P-101.IMPELLER
- **Signature:** **npsh_margin LOW** (w3), suction_pressure LOW (w2), radial_vibration HIGH (w2), head_ratio LOW (w1), discharge_pressure LOW (w1), flow LOW (w1)
- **Physics:** When the absolute pressure at the impeller eye approaches the fluid's vapour pressure (NPSHa approaching NPSH3), vapour bubbles form and then collapse violently in higher-pressure regions. The implosions cause broadband vibration and noise, pit the vanes, and vapour blocking the passages breaks down the developed head and flow.
- **Root causes:** RC-STRAINER-CLOGGED, RC-LOW-TANK-LEVEL, RC-HIGH-FLUID-TEMP, RC-SUCTION-VALVE-THROTTLED, RC-RUNOUT
- **Propagates to:** FM-IMPELLER-WEAR, FM-SEAL-LEAK

### FM-BEARING - Drive-end bearing degradation (HIGH)

- **Occurs in:** P-101.BRG-DE
- **Signature:** **bearing_temperature HIGH** (w3), radial_vibration HIGH (w2)
- **Physics:** Loss of the lubricant film, contamination or fatigue spalling increases rolling friction, raising bearing temperature; defects on races or rolling elements generate impacts that raise radial vibration. Higher temperature degrades the lubricant further, a self-accelerating loop.
- **Root causes:** RC-LUBRICATION, RC-LUBE-CONTAMINATION, RC-BEARING-FATIGUE

### FM-MISALIGNMENT - Pump-motor shaft misalignment (MEDIUM)

- **Occurs in:** P-101.COUPLING
- **Signature:** **axial_vibration HIGH** (w3), radial_vibration HIGH (w1.5), bearing_temperature HIGH (w1)
- **Physics:** The coupling is forced to flex every revolution, producing cyclic reaction forces that are characteristically strong in the axial direction (1x and 2x running speed) and add load to the bearings, which raises bearing temperature.
- **Root causes:** RC-INSTALL-MISALIGN, RC-PIPE-STRAIN, RC-SOFT-FOOT
- **Propagates to:** FM-BEARING, FM-SEAL-LEAK

### FM-IMBALANCE - Rotor / impeller imbalance (MEDIUM)

- **Occurs in:** P-101.IMPELLER
- **Signature:** **radial_vibration HIGH** (w3), **npsh_margin NORMAL** (w2), axial_vibration NORMAL (w1.5), bearing_temperature NORMAL (w2), head_ratio NORMAL (w1.5)
- **Physics:** The offset mass creates a centrifugal force F = m*e*omega^2 rotating at 1x running speed, giving predominantly radial vibration. Hydraulic performance (head, NPSH) is unaffected, which distinguishes imbalance from cavitation: while the NPSH margin is low, vibration cannot be attributed to imbalance.
- **Root causes:** RC-IMPELLER-FOULING, RC-IMPELLER-DAMAGE
- **Propagates to:** FM-BEARING

### FM-SEAL-LEAK - Mechanical seal leakage (HIGH)

- **Occurs in:** P-101.SEAL
- **Signature:** **seal_leak HIGH** (w3), seal_chamber_temperature HIGH (w1)
- **Physics:** The seal relies on a thin fluid film between rotating and stationary faces. Face wear, thermal distortion or heat-checking (e.g. after dry running) and elastomer degradation open the gap; leakage trips the leak detector and frictional heat raises the seal-chamber temperature.
- **Root causes:** RC-SEAL-FACE-WEAR, RC-ELASTOMER-DEGRADATION

### FM-DRY-RUN - Dry running (loss of suction) (CRITICAL)

- **Occurs in:** P-101
- **Signature:** **flow LOW_LOW** (w3), **discharge_pressure LOW** (w2), motor_current LOW (w2), head_ratio LOW (w1), seal_chamber_temperature HIGH (w1.5)
- **Physics:** Without liquid the impeller cannot develop head: discharge pressure collapses toward suction pressure and the head ratio falls to near zero. The motor only supplies mechanical losses, so current drops well below normal. The seal faces lose their lubricating and cooling film and overheat within minutes.
- **Root causes:** RC-STRAINER-CLOGGED, RC-EMPTY-TANK, RC-SUCTION-VALVE-CLOSED, RC-LOSS-OF-PRIME
- **Propagates to:** FM-SEAL-LEAK

### FM-DEADHEAD - Deadheading (blocked discharge) (CRITICAL)

- **Occurs in:** P-101
- **Signature:** **flow LOW_LOW** (w3), **discharge_pressure HIGH** (w3), motor_current LOW (w2), seal_chamber_temperature HIGH (w1), head_ratio NORMAL (w1)
- **Physics:** The pump operates at shut-off: pressure rises to the shut-off head while flow is ~zero. For a radial centrifugal pump absorbed power is minimum at shut-off, so motor current FALLS (it does not rise). All absorbed power heats the trapped liquid, so seal-chamber temperature climbs and the liquid can eventually flash to vapour.
- **Root causes:** RC-DISCHARGE-VALVE-CLOSED, RC-CHECK-VALVE-STUCK, RC-DISCHARGE-BLOCKED

### FM-IMPELLER-WEAR - Hydraulic degradation (impeller / wear-ring wear) (MEDIUM)

- **Occurs in:** P-101.IMPELLER
- **Signature:** **head_ratio LOW** (w3), **npsh_margin NORMAL** (w3), **motor_current NORMAL** (w2), discharge_pressure LOW (w1), flow LOW (w1)
- **Physics:** Vane erosion and opened wear-ring clearances increase internal recirculation from discharge back to suction, so the pump develops less head than its curve at the measured flow (head ratio < 1). NPSH margin stays healthy and the motor stays loaded, which separates wear from cavitation and from loss of suction.
- **Root causes:** RC-IMPELLER-DAMAGE, RC-WEAR-RING-WEAR

### FM-OVERLOAD - Motor overload (HIGH)

- **Occurs in:** P-101.MOTOR
- **Signature:** **motor_current HIGH** (w3), motor_winding_temperature HIGH (w2)
- **Physics:** Motor current tracks shaft load. For a radial centrifugal pump absorbed power rises with flow, so running far right on the curve (runout) overloads the motor; mechanical drag, a denser fluid or a low/unbalanced supply voltage do the same. The extra I^2R losses raise the winding temperature toward the insulation limit.
- **Root causes:** RC-RUNOUT, RC-MECH-BINDING, RC-SUPPLY-VOLTAGE, RC-DENSE-FLUID
